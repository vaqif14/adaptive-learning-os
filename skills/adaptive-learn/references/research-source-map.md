## Design interpretation

- Meta informed Study Intent / interaction semantics.
- Google informed challenge gates, artifact/performance evidence, workflow/playbook thinking, governance, and system observability.
- Stanford informed cognitive delegation and AI-use policy.
- Scott Young informed curriculum authority, practice coverage, and verifiability-by-construction.
- Habr/Craftum acted strongly as learning-vs-productivity anti-patterns.
- ITWeek informed Source Rights / Provenance separation from epistemic trust.
- Pedsovet informed accessibility and assessment-integrity separation.
- GigaChat informed domain-sensitive multimodality, authentic environment, and simulator-fidelity limits.
- The video reinforced protected cognition and AI-as-augmentor rather than replacement.

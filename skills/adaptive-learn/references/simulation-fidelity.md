# Simulation Fidelity & Authentic Environment

AI simulation is useful but evidence claims are bounded by fidelity.

Fidelity dimensions:

- cognitive;
- social;
- physical;
- temporal;
- authentic-environment status.

An AI language dialogue may validate grammar or conversational practice but cannot automatically prove robustness to spontaneous human speech, accent variation, social pressure, or real-world consequences.

Domains may require authentic environments for far-transfer claims.
